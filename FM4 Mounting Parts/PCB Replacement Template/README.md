# FM4 PCB replacement panel — Rev A

This is the first **photo-derived** laser-cutting template for replacing the
original FM4 PCB panel while retaining the original chassis.

## Files

- `FM4 PCB Replacement Panel - Rev A.svg` — 1:1 SVG in millimetres, suitable
  for most laser software.
- `FM4 PCB Replacement Panel - Rev A.dxf` — DXF alternative, units set to mm.
- `Photo reference - original PCB panel.jpg` — source photograph used for the
  initial trace.

## Dimensions and hole sizes

The outside panel is **296 × 173.5 mm**. The drawing uses:

- Red `CUT_3P5MM` layer: outer profile and chassis screw/fixing holes —
  **3.5 mm diameter** for fitting adjustment.
- Green `CUT_3MM` layer: five larger connector holes — **3 mm diameter**.
- Blue `CUT_2MM` layer: four small silver-pin holes and the matching smaller
  pin holes by the five-hole connector group — **2 mm diameter**.

## Important: test before cutting the final panel

The outside dimensions and hole diameters are confirmed measurements. The
individual hole positions are an initial trace from a single photograph and
are therefore deliberately marked **Rev A**. Print or laser-cut a card,
acrylic or scrap-MDF test at 100% scale, sit it in the chassis and correct any
hole centres before committing to a metal or finished panel.

Do not allow laser software to resize the SVG or DXF. Import at 1:1 and verify
that the outer dimensions read 296.0 × 173.5 mm.
